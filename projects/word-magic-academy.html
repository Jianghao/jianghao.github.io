Word Magic Academy

单词魔法学院

Completed2026 Link ↗

A colourful, multi-page English word-learning academy built for a young learner — with browse, flashcard, quiz and spelling modes across themed journeys.
为小小学习者 Rainbow 打造的彩色多页面英语单词学习学院——支持浏览、闪卡、测验、拼写四种模式，覆盖多个主题旅程。

Word Magic Academy is a playful, multi-page English word-learning system I built for Rainbow — a young learner. Every screen is designed to make vocabulary practice feel like a game rather than a drill.

Modes

Four ways to practise the same words, so a child can move between them as the mood strikes:

  • Browse — flip through words with their meanings at a comfortable pace
  • Flashcards — quick review with instant feedback
  • Quiz — test and track mastery
  • Spelling — type the word, learning to spell as well as recognise

Journeys

The vocabulary is organised into themed journeys — dedicated learning journeys, months, travel, and everyday vocabulary — each with its own illustrated pages and AI-generated study posters that give every word a memorable visual home.

Tech

Zero-dependency HTML/CSS/JS with a shared word bank (words.json) and a bright, kid-friendly palette — pastel cards, animated floating orbs, and a rounded, playful typeface. It runs entirely in the browser from static files.
