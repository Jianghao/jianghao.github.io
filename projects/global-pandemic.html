Project · 2020–2025

Global Pandemic

全球疫情

Ongoing2020–2025

A research programme using geospatial big data to understand how a pandemic moves through people — their sentiment, mobility, economy and the measures used to contain it.
一个利用地理空间大数据理解疫情如何在人群中传播的科研项目——从情绪、人口流动、经济到防疫措施。

Global Pandemic is a research programme that turned the COVID-19 pandemic into a planetary natural experiment. Using geospatial big data — geotagged social-media text, mobility signals and epidemiological modelling — it asks how a virus moves through people: how it reshapes expressed emotion, daily mobility and economic life, and how public-health measures bend those curves.

Themes

Emotions and society. From the global collapse and rebound of expressed sentiment during the pandemic, to fine-grained changes in daily-life fear perception.

Economic recovery. A large-scale field experiment in China showing how to encourage the resumption of economic activity after lockdowns.

Public-health measures. Disentangling how non-pharmaceutical interventions and vaccination shaped COVID-19 trajectories across Europe, how measures zeroed out successive SARS-CoV-2 variants, and which resumption strategies could flatten reopening-driven outbreaks.

Mobility and behaviour. Measuring Chinese mobility during the pandemic from geotagged social-media data, and how emotions shifted — including visiting green spaces — after the lifting of zero-COVID policies.

A natural experiment for the environment. Lockdowns offered a rare experiment to quantify human contributions to near-surface temperature inversions.
