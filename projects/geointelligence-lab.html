Project · 2023–2026

GeoIntelligence Lab

地理智能实验室

Ongoing2023–2026

Building foundation models and AI methods for geospatial prediction and decision support.
面向地理预测与决策支持，构建地理空间基础模型与人工智能方法。

GeoIntelligence Lab is an ongoing effort to bring modern foundation models to geographic problems — from mapping and prediction to climate and public-health decision support.

Research themes

  • Geospatial foundation models that can be fine-tuned for many downstream tasks rather than rebuilt for each one.
  • "Intelligent geography": coupling deep learning with spatial statistics so that predictions remain interpretable and uncertainty-aware.
  • Applications in climate risk, wildfire exposure, and urban sustainability.

Status

Active. New results are written up as they land, and the platform components are open-sourced progressively.
