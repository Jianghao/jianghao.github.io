Project · 2024–present

Global Wildfire

全球野火

Ongoing2024–present

A research programme on how wildfire smoke and the expanding wildland–urban interface shape human exposure to fire-related risk — and what it costs to mitigate.
一个研究野火烟雾与不断扩张的野火–城市界面如何影响人类暴露于火灾风险、以及缓解代价的科研项目。

Global Wildfire is a research programme on how wildfire smoke and the expanding wildland–urban interface shape human exposure to fire-related risk. As fires grow larger and people live closer to flammable landscapes, the programme asks: how much are we exposed, who bears the burden, and can clean indoor air offer a practical shield?

Themes

Expanding fire risk. The global wildland–urban interface — where houses meet wildfire-prone vegetation — is growing, drawing more people into the path of fire in the 21st century.

Indoor exposure. Because people spend most of their time indoors, wildfire smoke reaches us largely through our homes. We quantify how indoor wildfire-PM2.5 exposure is distributed unevenly across the world — and what it would cost to clean the air.

Mitigation and health. Indoor air purification emerges as a cost-effective way to reduce the health toll of smoke, with benefits far exceeding the costs in many regions.

Collaboration
