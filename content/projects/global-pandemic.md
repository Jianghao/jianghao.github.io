Global Pandemic is a research programme that turned the COVID-19 pandemic into a
planetary natural experiment. Using geospatial big data — geotagged social-media
text, mobility signals and epidemiological modelling — it asks how a virus moves
through people: how it reshapes **expressed emotion**, **daily mobility** and
**economic life**, and how **public-health measures** bend those curves.

## Themes

**Emotions and society.** From the global collapse and rebound of expressed sentiment
during the pandemic, to fine-grained changes in daily-life fear perception.

**Economic recovery.** A large-scale field experiment in China showing how to
encourage the resumption of economic activity after lockdowns.

**Public-health measures.** Disentangling how non-pharmaceutical interventions and
vaccination shaped COVID-19 trajectories across Europe, how measures zeroed out
successive SARS-CoV-2 variants, and which resumption strategies could flatten
reopening-driven outbreaks.

**Mobility and behaviour.** Measuring Chinese mobility during the pandemic from
geotagged social-media data, and how emotions shifted — including visiting green
spaces — after the lifting of zero-COVID policies.

**A natural experiment for the environment.** Lockdowns offered a rare experiment to
quantify human contributions to near-surface temperature inversions.
