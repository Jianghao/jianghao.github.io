Global Wildfire is a research programme on how wildfire smoke and the expanding
wildland–urban interface shape **human exposure** to fire-related risk. As fires
grow larger and people live closer to flammable landscapes, the programme asks: how
much are we exposed, who bears the burden, and can clean indoor air offer a practical
shield?

## Themes

**Expanding fire risk.** The global wildland–urban interface — where houses meet
wildfire-prone vegetation — is growing, drawing more people into the path of fire in
the 21st century.

**Indoor exposure.** Because people spend most of their time indoors, wildfire smoke
reaches us largely through our homes. We quantify how indoor wildfire-PM<sub>2.5</sub>
exposure is distributed unevenly across the world — and what it would cost to clean
the air.

**Mitigation and health.** Indoor air purification emerges as a cost-effective way to
reduce the health toll of smoke, with benefits far exceeding the costs in many
regions.
