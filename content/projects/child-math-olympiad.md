Child Math Olympiad is a collection of interactive Chinese-language maths
exercises for primary-school children. Each practice set encourages children to
try a problem themselves before gradually revealing hints and a full solution.

## Learning by trying

- Practice covers speed, meeting and pursuit problems, parity, logical reasoning,
  scheduling, river crossings and balance-scale puzzles.
- Three difficulty levels move from warm-ups to more challenging applications.
- Tiered hints lead to step-by-step solutions, diagrams and answer checks.
- Explanations use primary-school reasoning: diagrams, invariants and simple
  arithmetic, rather than equations.

## Progress that stays with the learner

Children can write drafts in an on-page notebook and mark problems as solved to
earn stars. Names, drafts and progress are saved in the local browser so practice
can continue on the next visit.

## Offline practice

The overview and exercise pages are static HTML files that can be opened directly
in a browser without an internet connection.
