Word Magic Academy is a playful, multi-page English word-learning system I built
for Rainbow — a young learner. Every screen is designed to make vocabulary practice
feel like a game rather than a drill.

## Modes

Four ways to practise the same words, so a child can move between them as the mood
strikes:

- **Browse** — flip through words with their meanings at a comfortable pace
- **Flashcards** — quick review with instant feedback
- **Quiz** — test and track mastery
- **Spelling** — type the word, learning to spell as well as recognise

## Journeys

The vocabulary is organised into themed journeys — dedicated learning journeys,
months, travel, and everyday vocabulary — each with its own illustrated pages and
AI-generated study posters that give every word a memorable visual home.

## Tech

Zero-dependency HTML/CSS/JS with a shared word bank (`words.json`) and a bright,
kid-friendly palette — pastel cards, animated floating orbs, and a rounded,
playful typeface. It runs entirely in the browser from static files.
