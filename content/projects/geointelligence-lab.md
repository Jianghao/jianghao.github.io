GeoIntelligence Lab is an ongoing effort to bring modern foundation models to
geographic problems — from mapping and prediction to climate and public-health
decision support.

## Research themes

- Geospatial foundation models that can be fine-tuned for many downstream tasks
  rather than rebuilt for each one.
- "Intelligent geography": coupling deep learning with spatial statistics so that
  predictions remain interpretable and uncertainty-aware.
- Applications in climate risk, wildfire exposure, and urban sustainability.

## Status

Active. New results are written up as they land, and the platform components are
open-sourced progressively.
