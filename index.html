Research

Turning spatial data into understanding of people & planet

My work sits at the intersection of geographic information science, computational social science, and Earth & environmental research.

01

Geospatial big data & spatial statistics

Turning mobile-phone signalling, social-media, remote-sensing and human-mobility data into reliable, analysis-ready evidence — including principled methods for scaling and downscaling Earth-science and population data.

02

Human behaviour, environment & health

Quantifying at global scale how the environment — air pollution, extreme heat, wildfire smoke, and even urban night light — shapes human sentiment, mobility, health and well-being, and revealing who bears the greatest burden.

03

Geographic intelligence & AI

Deep learning, foundation models and “intelligent geography” for prediction, mapping and decision support across climate, public health, wildfire risk and urban sustainability.
